Pinball Dojo Privacy Policy

November 13, 2026

On-device game data

Pinball Dojo saves settings, high scores, and best belt rank on your Apple Watch using Apple’s app preferences. The game operates offline without an account, advertising, analytics, or tracking. The app sends no game data to NinjaTom Apps.

Support email

If you email support, NinjaTom Apps receives your email address and the details you choose to share. NinjaTom Apps uses that information to respond to your request.

Contact

For privacy questions about Pinball Dojo, email support@ninjatomapps.com.
