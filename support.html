Pinball Dojo Support

Play the Master Dojo table on Apple Watch with watchOS 10 or later.

Digital Crown controls

  • Crown Tilt. Turn the Digital Crown to nudge the table. Tap either side of the screen to fire that flipper.
  • Crown Flippers. Turn the Digital Crown left or right to fire the corresponding flipper, then return toward center to release it.

New balls launch automatically after a short pause. You can also tap the lower launcher area when the ball is ready.

Settings

Open Settings to change the Crown mode or sensitivity, turn off haptics, or enable Reduced Motion to cut pulses and flashes.

Contact

Email support@ninjatomapps.com with your Apple Watch model, watchOS version, game version, and the steps that led to the issue.
